The ring-buffer page of my blog has a figure with all eight slots, a head pointer, a tail pointer and a count, and a stepper under it with six steps. Readers say they lose track. Each step's text reads like "the head pointer moves right", "the tail pointer stays", "the count goes up by one". The figure re-draws the slots in a different arrangement in steps 4 to 6 because the layout was easier to fit. Both pointers are drawn in the same colour. What is wrong, and what would you change?
